## Summary of R2: time budgets, Stats and a clearer app (2026-10-28, cloud session)

**What works.** The time you give a goal now decides its plan. You give it as a total ("20 hours"), a rhythm for a length ("10 h a week for 6 months", "6 h a day for 2 weeks") or per lecture ("2 h per lecture"); it always resolves to one true total. Code spends each goal's remaining time on ideas, most useful first (80/20): with little time, many ideas at overview depth; with more, deeper. An idea two goals need (calculus for physics and for deep learning) is learned once, paid once (cost split), never probed again, and goals assume it from then on. The plan re-fits after every session, so you never lose what you know. Each goal shows ideas ready against its plan, time against its total, this week against its rhythm, time per lecture, sessions left, and a pace verdict that speaks only when it is clear. A new goal is a four-step flow (goal, material, time, plan) ending in a plan preview you approve, with the time slider re-planning live. Everything you do is logged (time spans, sessions, lookups, depth changes, budget changes), and a new **Stats** tab turns it into plain sentences and charts, filterable by range, goal and topic. ## Decisions

Record choices the design did not settle, newest first: date, decision, reason.

- 2026-10-29 — The map as a living graph (R3 §7). The layered layout (`ui/lib/mapLayout.ts`) is gone; `ui/lib/mapGraph.ts` (pure, tested) builds the graph for `d3-force` 3.0.0 (ISC; its dependencies d3-dispatch, d3-quadtree and d3-timer are ISC; `@types/d3-force` MIT, dev only). A chain with several premises keeps a junction node (half a layer before its conclusion). Forces: links (distance as set, junction links 45% of it; strength 0.05–1), repel (−25 to −325, junctions −8, out to 700), centre (x/y pull 0.02–0.2), collide (radius + 3), and for "Prerequisites left to right" an x pull of 0.3 to (layer − half the deepest layer) × 1.6 × link distance. 300 warm-up ticks on first open, then live. Radius 3 + 1.6·√(ideas built on it), plus 0.35 per depth level for owned ideas, × idea size. Labels are placed greedily by priority (selected 1000, hovered 900, neighbours 500, matches 400, then 10 + 4 per dependent + a little for learnable and owned) and skipped when they would overlap another label or an overlay. Fit keeps 180 px clear at the top (title and search), 64 px at the bottom (legend) and the open card or view panel; after a filter or flow change the camera follows the layout until it settles (alpha < 0.08) or the learner moves it. Trackpad scroll pans, pinch or ⌘-scroll or a mouse wheel notch zooms. Full screen uses the Fullscreen API on the map element (no IPC). Settings live in renderer `localStorage` (`frontier.map.settings`, read through `mergeSettings` so old or partial values fall back to the defaults); they are a per-device convenience. Topics colour the first six clusters from `core/clusters.ts`, the rest muted. Replay how it grew orders ideas by their first passing evidence (`firstPasses`, new `MapNodeView.learnedAt`), the rest after, over about 6 s. Frame time with 574 synthetic plus 35 seed ideas under xvfb: 16.7 ms median and p95 while settling, zooming, hovering and switching the flow. Also fixed: separators between linked ideas in the idea card now follow the last word instead of drifting to the right edge in a narrow column.
- 2026-10-29 — Simplified marks and going deeper (R3 §6). A goal's total is now its budget plus `goals.extra_minutes` (migration 10), added only by going deeper; the learner's own entry is never rewritten. `deeperMinutes`: per simplified idea, `ideaMinutes(kind, current depth, ideal)` × learner speed, rounded up to 5 (at least 5). `goDeeper` adds that, clears the marks (approved depth back to ideal) and logs `budget.changed` with the ideas; it refuses while a session is open. `session.start` takes `focus`: a chosen plan with no planner call (the ideas at their ideal depth, after any unowned premises, dependency order, cut to 45 minutes; deepening stages when already explained). Marks: a small bar under the dot and a tooltip line in the plan dots, a line and "Go deeper" per idea in the goal's skills, a summary with "Go deeper on all" in the progress panel (worded against the exam for assessed goals), "Simplified for <goal>" with "Go deeper" on the map and lookup card, and a count on Today's goal row.
- 2026-10-29 — Re-maps (R3 §5). Gaps (`goals.gaps`, migration 9) come from the prober's new `gaps` list and from the tutor's `missingBackground` on a failed answer other than a prediction; a gap named before (case-insensitive) is not added again. `remapNeed` (pure): under 1 hour in all, nothing; within 25% of the hours left (or no budget), `fill`; otherwise `simplify`. The goal worker moved to `services/goalWorker.ts`; `nextGoalStep` returns `remap` for an active compiled goal when the learner asked, or when there is no pending proposal and new gaps call for one. `proposeRemap` runs the compiler on the whole map with a re-mapping task (fill, simplify, or asked), at least one capability, and `backgroundGoal` only then; the result is stored as `goals.remap` (state ready) and the gaps are marked proposed. `remapView` previews the new plan by applying the proposal inside a savepoint that is always rolled back (`dryRun`), so the preview code is shared. Approving replaces every capability (mastery included) and approves the new plan's depths; "Keep my map" drops the proposal and its gaps stay proposed. Handlers refresh the goal worker after a probe and when a session finishes or is ended. Goals without a level get "Draw a map for your time". "Start it as a goal" creates the background goal as a draft with its hours as a total.
- 2026-10-29 — The session fits each idea (R3 §4). A lesson step carries its `aim` (the deepest planned depth an active goal holds; 2 for a premise no goal targets), `fromDepth`, `minutes` (the cost model × learner speed, at most 30) and `simplified`; `StepMode`, tiers in steps and `modeFor` are gone, and steps saved before R3 are read as aims (index 1, teach 2, derive 3). Stages by aim: 1 index; 2 predict, explain, check, compress; 3 adds derive and connect; 4+ adds use; deepening from 2+ is predict, explain, then the deeper check. The aim's question is index, check, derive or use. Check-off replaces "two easy answers skip to compress": strong = score ≥ 0.85 and confidence fairly sure or more (or none); a strong prediction or two strong answers in a row jump to the aim's question; passing it strongly (or as the second strong answer) ends the idea; scoring under 0.7 on a test-out resumes after the stage it jumped from. "I know this, test me" (`LearnerResponse.testMe`) jumps from a prediction or explanation without grading it. Time box: allowance = step minutes within 10–30; minutes come from the activity log for this session plus what a carried idea had in the session it came from. Simplify (once per idea) when the tutor's new `missingBackground` is set on a failed answer other than a prediction, or after two failures in a row or a worked example once 60% of the allowance is used: `simplifyIdea` caps the idea for this goal only at 2 with the reason and caps the goal's direct dependents planned at 3+ at 2 ("Simplified with X"), then a fresh explain card at the intuition level. Wrap-up at the allowance: compress if still ahead, else end. An idea that ends below its aim (aim 2+) is capped at the depth reached (at least 1) with "Learned to … within its half hour; the plan asked …". Caps live on `targets.approved_depth` with `targets.simplified` (migration 8); a new time keeps simplified caps. Sessions are cut to 45 minutes of step cost in the planner's order (at least one step; a carried idea's minutes count first). The tutor now gets the goal's level, scope, assessment and focus, the aim, the simplified reason and which premises were learned as an intuition only.
- 2026-10-29 — Plans sized to the time (R3 §3). The "15 hours for 10 hours" screenshot was a UI bug: the time picker showed 10 h for a goal with no budget (pre-R2 goals, or a resumed draft) but sent `budget: null`, so the preview planned every target in full; every time control now shows and sends a real budget (`ui/lib/budgetDefaults.ts`: a course 4 h a week to its date or for twelve weeks, anything else 10 h). Cost per level (all in) 7/18/5/8/8 minutes, so recognizing 7, explaining 25 and deriving 30 in all; terms half; a premise no plan holds costs owning it (25). Value per level 0.35/1/0.45/0.25/0.15: explaining is worth more per minute than recognizing, so an idea climbs to its ideal depth once its first level is bought, and what does not fit is left out rather than skimmed. Core targets (the compiler's `core`) weigh 2×. "In depth" means planned at 2 or more, "overview" planned at 1. The compiler is asked for `round(minutes ÷ 30 × 1.2)` ideas (4–80): 24 for 10 h, 5 for 2 h. On a first compile it must give `level` (intuition, working, rigorous) and `scope`; the prompt's Time and Depth sections were rewritten (fewer ideas understood beat many recognized; ideas whole and at most half an hour; written at the chosen level), reversing R2's "do not shrink depths for time". An optional "What do you already know about it?" (`goals.background`) feeds the compiler. Each target stores `approved_depth`: set from the plan when the learner approves a draft, changes the time (caps cleared, re-planned, set again) or a recompile adds targets; automatic re-plans cap ideal depths at it (previews of a new time do not). Pre-R3 targets have none and stay uncapped. Tiers follow the planned depth: 2+ internalize, 1 index, 0 archive (`INTERNALIZE_SHARE` is gone). Planning treats an idea the goal only asks to recognize as done once recognized (`doneSet`), so it is not picked again and its dependents unlock. Recompiles now see every target with its ideal depth, left-out ones included.
- 2026-10-29 — Plan dots (R3 §2): the hover tooltip that Stats charts used moved to `ui/components/tooltip.tsx` and is shared. A dot's tooltip gives the idea's name, what the plan does with it ("In depth: derive", "Overview: recognize. - 2026-10-29 — Esc to stop (R3 §1). A `CommitGate` (`llm/cancel.ts`) gives the request's first model call an abort signal; the services write nothing before that call, so stopping leaves no trace, and once it has answered the request commits and runs to the end (`session.cancel`/`probe.cancel` then return false). The gate rejects at once and lets the provider wind down in the background, so the answer is back in the field within a frame. Only answers to questions can be stopped (graded cards, free recall, the probe); reading cards and "Begin" have nothing to edit. A session refuses a second answer while one is in flight. Drafts are now kept until the answer is accepted (before, the field was cleared on send, so any error lost the answer). Practice is unchanged: its solution shows on submit and grading runs in the background. The Agent SDK provider now honours an already-aborted signal and reports a stop as `cancelled` instead of "ended without calling the submit tool"; the queue drops a stopped waiter.

- 2026-10-28 — Time budgets (R2, design §3.4).

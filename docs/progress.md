## Decisions

Record choices the design did not settle, newest first: date, decision, reason.

- 2026-11-11 (R7 A10): Library. (1) Files dropped anywhere on the window are added to the Library and read (`ui/lib/fileDrop.ts`, `library.addPaths`); a hint covers the window while files are dragged over it; a goal's material step keeps the files dropped on it; what could not be added is said in a notice wherever the learner is. (2) "Stop reading" (`IngestionService.stop`): the model call in flight is dropped through its abort signal, parts already read stay, the dropped part goes back to waiting with no attempt counted, and the source is marked like one cut off by a quit (status failed, "You stopped the reading. Run it again to read the rest."), so goals that wait for it move on; the Library calls it "Stopped", not failed. A source stopped while it waits its turn is not read at all. (3) Time left (`secondsLeft`): the length of the unread parts times a pace that starts at one second per 1,000 characters (at least 15 s a part) and follows what parts actually took in this run; the Library asks again every four seconds while something is read and says "About 40 seconds left", "a minute", or that it is taking longer than usual. It is a guess, not a promise. (4) "Open original" opens the stored copy in its usual app. Also: a failed part's reason is shown in plain words (it was the raw answer of the model service and made the page scroll sideways), and the three add buttons sit on one row under "Sources". Checked in the running app.

- 2026-11-11 (R7 A1, fix): The plan list could read differently each time. `roadmap` broke ties between ideas of the same weight by their ids, which are random, so two ideas could swap places from one look to the next (and a test failed now and then: the commit "R7 A6" went out with that test red). Ties now go by the order the ideas are given in (planned depth, then the goal's depth, then name). The whole suite passed three runs in a row afterwards.

- 2026-11-11 (R7 A6): Stats tidy-up. End labels of a line chart (`ui/lib/chartLabels.ts`) are 15 px apart, stay inside the plot (a stack of lines ending at zero moves up instead of running into the axis) and take their line's colour when there is more than one line. "How sure, how right" shows only the confidences that were used. A chart is replaced by one sentence when it would say less than the sentence: "Time per week" with under two weeks of study, "What you know" until ideas were learned on two days, "When you study" until three hours of the day have five minutes or more. Checked in the running app.

- 2026-11-11 (R7 A5): Flashcard review comfort. `cards.undoReview` removes a card's latest review when it is a Cards-screen rating from the last ten minutes (`removeLatestScreenReview`); a review a session or practice gave is never removed. This is the one place a review is deleted: a mis-pressed key is not something the learner did, and a card's schedule is the replay of its reviews, so removing the row is the whole undo. The queue then shows that card first, with its answer showing. The position counts this sitting's ratings ("Card 3 of 12"; a card rated Again comes back and is counted again). The end line counts each card once, by its last rating. Checked in the running app. Design §2.6 and §10 updated.

- 2026-11-11 (R7 C5, first part): Misconceptions end. `core/bugs.ts` `hasEnded`: two direct passing answers about the idea (`reviewRole` direct, score at or above the pass mark) after the misconception was last seen, at least one of them 12 hours or more after. `services/bugs.ts` `afterAnswer` is now the one writer of the bugs table and replaces three copies of "seen before? mark it, else record it" (session, practice, probe): an answer that shows a misconception records or re-opens it, dated when the learner answered; an answer that shows none closes the idea's misconceptions that have ended. Until now `resolveBug` was never called, so the lists only grew. Matching is still by the exact sentence (recognising a repeat by meaning is for the next session). Design §5.2 updated.

- 2026-11-11 (R7 D26): The log. `backend/log.ts` `LogFile` appends to `frontier.log` beside `settings.json` (the app's own folder, not the data folder, so it survives a change of data folder); 1 MB, then one older file is kept. Written: the start (version, platform), every failed call from the window (`call <name>`), every failed model call with its role (`ThrottledProvider` tells the context; a call the learner stopped is not an error), every error notice and failed part of a source sent to the window (`AppContext.emit` now logs and forwards; the window's sink is `ctx.sink`), and uncaught exceptions and rejections in the main process (logged, the app keeps running). Entries carry the message and the top five stack lines, never learner text, prompts or keys. `app.showLog` reveals the file; Settings, Data has "Show log". Design §11.4 updated.

- 2026-11-11 (R7 B3): Add from a link. `library.addLink` takes an address (`core/links.ts` `parseLink`: https may be left off, only web addresses, no names or passwords in them, a page's "#part" dropped, every way of writing one video is one link). A page, PDF, Markdown or text file is fetched once (`ingest/link.ts`: the app's own name as user agent, 30 s, 60 MB at most, the system's network stack so a proxy applies), stored in the sources folder and read like a file (`IngestionService.addDocument`, which `addFile` now uses too). A video link returns `needs-transcript` with the title from the site's public embedding description (oEmbed), and the learner pastes the transcript. `transcriptCues` recognises a pasted transcript in any language (a time stamp alone on a line or at the start of one; at least four, running forward; the spelled-out stamp some pages add for screen readers is dropped) and `transcriptBlocks` keeps it by the minute with the time as the place; this applies to any pasted transcript, with or without a link. `npm run live:link -- <address>` shows what a link would give; run once here on an encyclopedia article (title and 17 parts read).
